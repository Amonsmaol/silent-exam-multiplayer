Keyboard

Hold
Press and hold anywhere, or hold Space, to ask Claude. The answer shows on your phone.
Type
Put the phone away and type the answer onto your paper. You can't ask the next question until it is written.
Release
Let go when you hear a creak or see a head turn. If they see the screen, it's over.
Look
Use the ◀ ▶ buttons or the arrow keys to look around the room. You can't watch the teacher while you're watching the others.
Phone
Battery and signal both matter. Weak signal slows answers, and a flat phone gives none. You get one power bank.
Leave
Ask to leave while she is at the board. In the bathroom you can use the phone freely, but you can't write, and she might follow.
Scanner
Some exams have a phone scanner sweep the room. Hold nothing while it passes.
Call
While holding your phone, call another student. If their phone isn't on silent it rings and the teacher turns on them. If the teacher comes for you, talk your way out.
Friends
Versus races the class. Co-op shares the clock. In Haunt, one player is the teacher and the rest try to finish.

Multiplayer

The class

Everyone sits the same exam, at the same time, in the same room. You will see each other's name tags when you look around.

Silent
Exam

You didn't study. Claude is in your pocket. Whoever is watching the room is not as distracted as they look. Neither are the other students.

Hold
Press and hold anywhere, or hold Space, to ask Claude. The answer shows on your phone.
Type
Put the phone away and type the answer onto your paper. You can't ask the next question until it is written.
Release
Let go when you hear a creak or see a head turn. If they see the screen, it's over.
Look
Use the ◀ ▶ buttons or the arrow keys to look around the room. You can't watch the teacher while you're watching the others.
Call
While holding your phone, call another student. If their phone isn't on silent it rings and the teacher turns on them. Silence yours first. If the teacher comes for you, talk your way out.
